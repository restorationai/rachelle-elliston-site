## Restoration emergencies common in Nellis AFB

The Las Vegas Valley's desert climate drives most of what we see out this way. Flat terrain near the base means standing water has nowhere fast to go. Add extreme summer heat that stresses roofing membranes and HVAC condensate lines, plus the occasional hard freeze that cracks exposed piping, and you get a steady mix of water intrusion calls alongside the usual appliance failures and slab leaks that show up in any Southern Nevada neighborhood with an aging supply line.

Water damage in this part of the valley often starts at a slab penetration, a failed water heater, or a roof seam that gave way during a monsoon downpour. We extract standing water, pull moisture out of drywall and subfloor, and document the loss for your carrier as we go.

Reconstruction closes out the job, whether that means a single room of new drywall and flooring or a full rebuild after a fire.
