Desert Valley Contracting Inc provides water damage, fire and smoke damage, mold remediation, biohazard cleanup, and reconstruction services for homeowners and property managers in Sandy Valley, NV. Because the community sits well outside the Las Vegas valley's municipal utility grid, most of the properties we work on here run on private wells and septic systems, which changes how a water loss gets diagnosed and dried compared to a tract home in town. ## Restoration emergencies common in Sandy Valley

Sandy Valley sits in a high desert basin between the Spring Mountains and the McCullough Range, and that geography drives most of the damage calls we see. Summer monsoon storms can dump a heavy, fast burst of rain on hardpan desert soil that doesn't absorb it, and runoff finds its way into crawlspaces and under slab perimeters faster than homeowners expect. Low humidity for most of the year keeps wood framing and drywall dry, but it also means a slow plumbing leak can sit undetected behind a wall for weeks before anyone notices a smell or a soft spot in the flooring. Well and septic systems add another failure point: a tank backup or a pressure tank leak is a different cleanup scope than a municipal sewer line, and we plan for that difference from the first walkthrough.

## Services we provide in Sandy Valley

Water damage in Sandy Valley often starts at a well pressure tank, a washing machine supply line, or a roof penetration that monsoon wind has worked loose, and because many homes here are on crawlspace or raised foundations, water can travel along floor joists before it ever shows upstairs. We extract standing water, pull moisture readings from the subfloor and framing, and dry the structure in place rather than guessing at what's wet.

Fire and smoke damage calls out here frequently trace back to wildland-adjacent brush fires or electrical faults in older manufactured housing wiring. We clear smoke odor from framing and insulation, not just surfaces, and coordinate structural repairs where fire has compromised framing members.

Mold remediation in this climate is usually tied to a water event rather than ambient humidity, since the desert air itself rarely supports mold growth on its own. Once moisture gets trapped behind paneling or under flooring, though, it colonizes fast, and we remove and treat affected materials rather than masking the problem with surface cleaning.

Biohazard cleanup, including septic backups and trauma or hazardous material situations, gets handled with the same documentation and containment standards we'd use anywhere, scaled to a property that may not have municipal sewer access to fall back on.

Reconstruction follows any of the above when drywall, subfloor, framing, or roofing has to come out. We handle that rebuild in-house so the property isn't passed between a mitigation crew and a separate contractor.

## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Sandy Valley

Most homes in Sandy Valley were built from the 1980s forward, a mix of manufactured housing and site-built ranch homes on large rural parcels, typically slab-on-grade or raised crawlspace foundations rather than basements. Plumbing is a mix of copper and PEX depending on the build era, paired with private well systems and septic tanks instead of municipal water and sewer, which affects both how a leak gets traced and how a cleanup gets sequenced. Soil here is desert sand and caliche hardpan, which sheds monsoon rainfall quickly rather than absorbing it, putting pressure on grading and foundation drainage during late-summer storms. As an unincorporated community, building and structural repair permits in Sandy Valley go through Clark County's building department rather than a city office, and HOA oversight is minimal to nonexistent given the area's large-lot, low-density character.
