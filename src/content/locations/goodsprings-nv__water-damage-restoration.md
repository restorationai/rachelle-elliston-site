## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Goodsprings

We start with moisture mapping, not guesswork, because in older Goodsprings construction the visible wet spot is rarely the whole story. Lath-and-plaster sections, adobe-style block, or additions tacked onto original mining-era buildings all hold and release water differently than standard drywall, so our IICRC-trained technicians adjust extraction and drying targets to the actual materials in the structure rather than a generic timeline. Standing water comes out first with truck-mount or portable extraction, then we set air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to the room's actual humidity load, which in a desert climate can swing hard between bone-dry daytime air and a soaked crawlspace after a storm. We track drying progress with moisture meters throughout the job and document readings for your insurance file, following the IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration so the drying is verified, not assumed.
